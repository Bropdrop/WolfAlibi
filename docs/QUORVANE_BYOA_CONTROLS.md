## Interpreting an interruption

Distinguish a draft owner grant, a committed admission, a recorded broker read, delivery to the worker, a completed callback and a signed terminal report. A later failure can leave only a prefix. Verify the surviving records against the originally retained reference. Reconcile a pending decision through the historical lookup supported by that authority. Do not re-dispatch to resolve an unknown result. An intact local chain cannot detect replacement of every copy and every trusted reference by their common administrator.
